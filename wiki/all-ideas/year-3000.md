# Year 3000

There can be a lot to be said about the year 3000. It is dark but it is beautiful. There has been lots of technological advancements, but also regressions. As AI became more powerful, the rise of [[the most powerful open source model]] really turned everything on its head. 

Who create this Open source model you say? It was an [[anonymous creator]]. No one knows who created it. Or even what the motives of building such an ai were. The only thing we know is that it created on a level that was impossible before it.
