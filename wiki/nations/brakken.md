# Brakken

Brakken is a fortified nation carved into the eastern face of the Thornwall Mountains. Where Leren values patience, Brakken prizes strength and decisive action. Their cities are built vertically — great towers and bridges spanning impossible chasms.

## Geography

Brakken occupies the rugged highlands beyond the Thornwall, a landscape of deep gorges, volcanic plateaus, and dense iron-pine forests. The nation's heart is the **Crucible Valley**, a geothermally active basin where the capital city of **Forgegate** rises in tiers of blackened steel and stone.

## Government

Brakken is ruled by the **Iron Compact**, a meritocratic council of forge-masters and war-scholars. Leadership is earned through demonstrated excellence — in craft, strategy, or governance. The current First Forge is **Commander Sael Drenn**, who has held the position for eleven years.

## Culture

Brakken culture revolves around the forge, both literal and metaphorical. Citizens are expected to master at least one craft by adulthood. Their famous **echo-steel**, an alloy that resonates at specific frequencies, is used in everything from architecture to communication.

## Relations

Relations with [Leren](/wiki/nations/leren) are complex. The two nations share the Thornwall border and have fought three wars over mining rights in the past century. A fragile trade agreement currently holds, with Brakken exchanging raw minerals for Leren's [Luminthread](/wiki/technology/luminthread) technology.
