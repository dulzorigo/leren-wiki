# Leren

Leren is the central nation of the known world, a vast territory stretching from the Pale Coasts in the west to the Thornwall Mountains in the east. It is the oldest continuous civilisation, and the one from which all others measure their calendars.

## Geography

The land of Leren is defined by three great features: the **Luminous Basin**, a fertile lowland fed by underground rivers that glow faintly at night; the **Thornwall Mountains**, a jagged range that forms the eastern border; and the **Pale Coasts**, white-sand shores that face the Endless Western Sea.

## Government

Leren is governed by the **Council of Threads**, a body of twelve elected weavers — not of cloth, but of policy. Each Thread represents a district and serves for a cycle of seven years. Decisions require the agreement of at least eight Threads, a system that has produced both remarkable stability and occasional paralysis.

## Culture

The people of Leren value patience, craftsmanship, and the preservation of knowledge above all else. Their architecture blends living plants with carved stone, a tradition known as **greenforging**. Every major city contains at least one Archive — a living library where knowledge is stored in crystalline structures grown from mineral-rich waters.

## Relations

Leren maintains cautious trade relations with [Brakken](/wiki/nations/brakken), though tensions over the Thornwall border persist. The nation's technological innovations, particularly in [Luminthread Weaving](/wiki/technology/luminthread), have made it both admired and envied.
