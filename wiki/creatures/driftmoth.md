# Driftmoth

Driftmoths are ethereal, bioluminescent creatures that inhabit the Luminous Basin of [Leren](/wiki/nations/leren). Ranging from the size of a human hand to nearly a metre in wingspan, they drift through the night air in slow, hypnotic patterns, their wings producing a soft cyan glow.

## Biology

Driftmoths feed on the mineral-rich vapours that rise from the underground rivers of the Luminous Basin. Their wings are covered in crystalline scales that absorb and re-emit light, creating their characteristic glow. Each moth produces a slightly different wavelength, and in large swarms, the combined effect is a shimmering aurora at ground level.

## Connection to Luminthread

The discovery of [Luminthread Weaving](/wiki/technology/luminthread) is said to have been inspired by observing Driftmoth wing structures under magnification. The crystalline lattice of their scales shares remarkable similarities with the manufactured Luminthread fibres, leading some scholars to theorise that the moths are themselves a product of the same ancient forces that created the glowing underground rivers.

## Cultural Role

Driftmoths are considered sacred in Leren. Harming one is taboo, and their appearance in large numbers is taken as an omen of good fortune. The annual **Drift Festival** celebrates the peak of moth activity during the warm season, with cities dimming their lights so the moths can be seen in their full splendour.
