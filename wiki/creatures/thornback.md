# Thornback

The Thornback is a large, heavily armoured reptilian creature found throughout the Thornwall Mountains and the highlands of [Brakken](/wiki/nations/brakken). They are solitary hunters, typically two to three metres in length, covered in overlapping plates of bone and mineral deposits that give them a stone-like appearance.

## Behaviour

Thornbacks are ambush predators. They can remain motionless for days, their mineral-crusted hide rendering them nearly indistinguishable from the rocky terrain. When prey approaches, they strike with explosive speed, using a barbed tail that can shatter stone.

## Ecology

Despite their fearsome reputation, Thornbacks play a vital role in the mountain ecosystem. Their burrowing creates cave systems that are later colonised by other species, and their mineral-rich droppings fertilise the thin mountain soil. The people of Brakken have learned to coexist with them, and some forge-masters use shed Thornback plates in their alloys.

## Cultural Significance

In Brakken culture, the Thornback represents resilience and patience — qualities they share with their Leren neighbours, though they would be reluctant to admit it. Young warriors traditionally undertake a **Thornwalk**, a solo journey through Thornback territory, as a rite of passage.
